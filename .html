DISCOVER ISLAM

5 PILLARS OF ISLAM

Faith/Shahada

Belief in the Unity of Allah & Prophet Muhammad ﷺ as Allah’s messenger.

Prayer/Salah

The act of worshipping Allah five times a day throughout your lifetime.

Alms/Zakat

Sharing a certain percentage of your wealth with less fortunate every year.

Fasting/Roza

Fasting from dawn to dusk for 30 days in the holy month of Ramadan.

Pilgrimage/Hajj

A spiritual duty that every Muslim must offer once in their life, if able.

Why Choose Us

At Ummat ka Bhala, we provide an exceptional learning experience for students of all levels.

Qualified Instructors

At Ummat ka Bhala’s Online Quran Academy, we take pride in our team of dedicated and experienced instructors. They are passionate about teaching and use proven methods to ensure an engaging and meaningful Quranic learning journey for every student.

Convenient Learning Options

We understand the demands of modern life and aim to provide flexible class schedules that fit seamlessly into your routine. Learn at your own pace, no matter where you are in the world.

Comprehensive Curriculum

Our carefully crafted curriculum covers all essential aspects of Quranic education, including Tajweed, Tafseer, Hadith, and Islamic jurisprudence. It ensures a steady and well-rounded progression for students at all levels.

Interactive and Modern Learning Experience

Using advanced online tools, Ummat ka Bhala’s Quran Academy offers a highly interactive learning environment. Engage in live discussions, ask questions, and receive instant feedback, making your educational experience both immersive and effective.

Affordable and Accessible Education

We are committed to making Quranic education accessible to everyone. Our academy offers affordable tuition fees, ensuring that learning the Quran is within reach for all members of the community.

Personalized Attention

Our small class sizes and one-on-one sessions allow instructors to give personalized attention to each student. This approach helps address individual learning needs and ensures a tailored educational experience.
